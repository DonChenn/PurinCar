PurinCar Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

PurinCar is a personal vehicle maintenance tracking app. This policy describes how your data is handled.

Data We Collect

  • Your Google account email and name (for sign-in only)
  • Vehicle information and maintenance records you enter

How We Use Your Data

Your data is stored in your personal Firebase account and is only accessible by you. It is never sold or shared with third parties.

Data Storage

Data is stored locally on your device and synced to Google Firebase Firestore under your unique user ID.

Contact

For any questions, contact the app developer directly.
